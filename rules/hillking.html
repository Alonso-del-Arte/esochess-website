King of the Hill Rules

All the rules of regular chess apply. Checkmate wins the game, but you can also win by placing your king in any one of the center squares: d4, e4, d5, e5.

Strategy suggestions

Even though getting your king in the center is the point of this variant, castling is still quite advisable, since you can also win by checkmate. If your opponent manages to drive your king out of his castled position, then remember that you can win by getting your king in the center.

Indeed we don't expect any players in the tournament to win with king in the center before the middlegame.
