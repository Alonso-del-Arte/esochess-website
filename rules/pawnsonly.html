Rules for Pawns Only at First

All the rules of regular chess apply. However, players are restricted in their initial moves, in that they may only move pawns until one of the following things happens:

  • A king is check, or
  • A pawn is promoted, or
  • All the pawns are stymied.

Once of those three things happens in a game, both players are released from the pawns only stricture, and may thereafter make whatever moves are allowed under the rules of the regular game. That includes castling, however inadvisable it might be in a game of this sort.
